# WESTLINE — Overview

## Business

WESTLINE is an international online surf shop inspired by California surf culture, selling surf equipment and lifestyle products — surfboards, wetsuits, fins, leashes, board bags, traction pads, wax, apparel, and accessories — across different price and quality tiers. Its key differentiator is the Surfboard Finder, a guided questionnaire that helps beginner-to-intermediate surfers find the right board.

## Goals & Engagement

Build a full V1 e-commerce surf store in 4 weeks, for the Moveo Academy Capstone. Solo engagement — one person covering both PM and Dev.
