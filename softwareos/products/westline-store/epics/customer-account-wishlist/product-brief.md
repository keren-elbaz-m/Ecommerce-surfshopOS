# Customer Account & Wishlist — Product Brief

> Epic: epic.md · Product: ../../mission.md

## Goals

Without accounts, shoppers can only guest-checkout (cart-checkout epic) and have no way to save favorites or see past orders — every visit starts from zero. Goal: let shoppers create an account, log in/out, manage their profile, view order history, and save products to a wishlist.

## User Stories

- As a shopper, I can sign up and log in/out so I have a persistent identity.
- As a shopper, I can manage my profile so my info stays current.
- As a shopper, I can view my order history so I can track past purchases.
- As a shopper, I can add/remove/view wishlist items so I can save products for later.

## Success Criteria

- A shopper can sign up, log in, edit their profile, see their past orders, and add/remove/view wishlist items — with all data persisted in Strapi and tied to their account.

## Out of Scope

- Surfboard Finder (Surfboard Finder epic)
- Seller dashboard / inventory management (Seller Dashboard epic)
- Social login
- Password reset via email (unless trivial with Strapi's Users & Permissions plugin)
