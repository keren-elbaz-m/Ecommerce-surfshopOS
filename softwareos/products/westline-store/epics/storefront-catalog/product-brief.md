# Storefront & Catalog — Product Brief

> Epic: epic.md · Product: ../../mission.md

## Goals

Without a catalog, customers can't discover or evaluate WESTLINE's products at all — there's no storefront. Goal: give shoppers a working way to browse the full catalog, narrow it down, inspect a product in detail, and add it to a cart, as the foundation every other V1 feature (checkout, wishlist, Surfboard Finder) builds on.

## User Stories

- As a shopper, I can browse the catalog so I can see what's available.
- As a shopper, I can search/filter/sort so I can narrow results to what fits me.
- As a shopper, I can view a product's detail page so I can evaluate it before buying.
- As a shopper, I can add a product to my cart so I can move toward checkout.

## Success Criteria

- A shopper can land on the catalog, search/filter/sort results, open a product detail page, and click Add to Cart, with the cart reflecting the added item — all against real Strapi-backed product data.

## Out of Scope

- Full cart page and checkout flow (Cart & Checkout epic)
- Customer accounts (Customer Account epic)
- Wishlist (Wishlist epic)
- Surfboard Finder (Surfboard Finder epic)
- Seller dashboard (Seller Dashboard epic)
