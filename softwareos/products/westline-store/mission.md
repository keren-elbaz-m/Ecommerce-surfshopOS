# Product Mission

> Customer: ../../customer/overview.md

## Problem

Beginner-to-intermediate surfers struggle to choose the right surf equipment — especially boards — without expert guidance. The Surfboard Finder solves this with a guided questionnaire, wrapped in a full online surf shop.

## Target Users

Surfers across all skill levels — the store carries equipment across a range of price and quality tiers to suit beginners through advanced surfers. The Surfboard Finder is aimed specifically at beginner-to-intermediate surfers (e.g. mid-20s, surfing 1-3x/week) who've moved past the total-beginner stage and want to buy their own gear but don't feel confident choosing by technical spec — the primary anchor persona, though the store itself isn't limited to them.

## Solution

The Surfboard Finder — a guided questionnaire that translates a surfer's experience and preferences into a confident board recommendation — layered on top of a full-catalog international surf shop spanning price and quality tiers.
